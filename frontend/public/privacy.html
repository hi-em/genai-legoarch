Privacy

Last updated 10 September 2026

lEgoarCh is a non-commercial academic project. It stores as little about you as it can while still giving you a collection that survives closing the browser.

You do not need an account

You can use the whole app without signing in — describe a building, watch a set solve and assemble, open the box, read the instructions, export the files. If you never sign in, nothing about you is stored on the server at all.

What is stored if you do sign in

Signing in with Google gives your saved sets somewhere to live. We store:

  • your name, email address and profile picture, as provided by Google;
  • the date you last signed in;
  • the LEGO sets you explicitly choose to save — the brick layout, the set name and description, and the small preview images shown on your shelf.

What is not stored

The prompts you type, the settings and sliders you change, how long a render took, what you looked at and did not save — none of it is recorded. There is no analytics service, no advertising, and no third-party tracking on this site.

Who can see it

Only you. Your sets are readable only by your own signed-in account, and the project authors, who administer the database. Nothing is sold, shared or published, and nothing is used to train any model.

Where it is kept

On Google Cloud (Firestore and Cloud Storage), in the europe-west4 region in the Netherlands. Signing in is handled by Google; this site never sees your password.

Deleting it

At any time, open the account menu in the top-right of the app and choose Delete my account. This immediately and permanently erases your account record and every set on your shelf. It is not a request queued for someone to action — the data is gone when the button finishes. There is no way to undo it.

You can also revoke this site's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, though that alone does not delete what is already stored — use the button for that.
